## What remains unresolved

first instrumented packet. The earlier profile did not record the numeric
notice counter or a unique writer route, so this packet cannot establish that
historical run's precise timing cause. Observation consumes CPU and can affect
a race; conclusions apply to these measured schedules. No case is rerun until
a desired warning appears or disappears, no warning field is removed, and no
historical variation allowance is added.

The different writer phases above are observed internal facts, not proof of a
public SDK defect. There is no justified production warning change from these
four matches. Original `host.turn-barrier.outcomes` remains unverified in the
latest full assessment.
